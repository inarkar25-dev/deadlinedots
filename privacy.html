Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Introduction

Deadline Dots is designed to be private by default. The app does not require an account and is built to function without collecting personal data.

Information we collect

Deadline Dots does not collect personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Account information
  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics data
  • Tracking data

Deadline data

Deadline titles, dates, progress, and preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

Widgets

Deadline Dots uses Apple App Groups to share deadline information locally between the main app and its Home Screen widget. This data is not sent to our servers.

Third-party services

Deadline Dots does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or data-collection SDKs.

Internet and cloud storage

Deadline Dots does not require cloud storage or a user account to function.

Children's privacy

Deadline Dots does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. Any updated policy will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: deadlinedots@gmail.com
